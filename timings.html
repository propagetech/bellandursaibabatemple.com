Darshan rhythm

Darshan & aarti timings

Daily worship schedule at Shri Sai Baba Temple, Bellandur - confirm with the temple office on festival days or if you are travelling from outside the city.

Opening hours

The temple is generally open from 6:00 AM until approximately 9:30 PM, with the main sanctum available for darshan between daily aartis. Opening and closing times may vary slightly by season or special observances - please confirm current hours with the temple office before your visit, especially on holidays.

Address: 109, Margosa Ave, Green Glen Layout, Bellandur, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560103 · Email: saibaba.bellandur@gmail.com

Daily aarti schedule

Four aartis mark the day’s rhythm of worship. Arrive a few minutes early to join comfortably.

Daily aarti schedule with brief descriptions
Aarti Time About
Kakada Aarti 6:30 AM Morning opening worship - lamps and prayers to begin the day’s darshan.
Madhyana Aarti 12:00 PM Midday aarti at the centre of the day - a calm pause for office-goers and neighbours.
Dhoopaarathi 6:30 PM Evening lamp and incense offering - the most attended aarti on Thursdays and busy days.
Shejaarathi 9:00 PM Night closing aarti - concluding the day’s worship before the temple winds down.

Best times to visit

  • Early morning (before Kakada) - quieter darshan for personal prayer before the day’s bustle.
  • Mid-morning after Kakada - suitable for families who prefer moderate crowds.
  • Weekday afternoons - generally calmer than Thursday evenings or festival days.
  • During aarti - join if you wish to experience collective bhajan and lamp offering; arrive early for seating.

Thursday callout

Thursday: Many devotees visit for evening darshan and special observances. Dhoopaarathi at 6:30 PM draws larger crowds - arrive early, use street parking thoughtfully, and follow volunteer directions.

Annadanam and community gatherings are often fuller on Thursdays. If you are visiting with elders, plan for extra queue time and consider morning darshan instead.

Festival guidance

On Guru Purnima, Ram Navami, Vijayadashami, and other observances, the temple may extend darshan hours, add special pooja, or adjust aarti sequence. Crowds and traffic near Green Glen Layout increase - allow extra travel time.

Check the festivals page for general guidance, and email saibaba.bellandur@gmail.com or ask the office for the confirmed schedule on your chosen date.

Elders & children

Volunteers often assist elders toward seating near the sanctum when space allows. There are steps at the entrance - please ask for help if needed. Supervise young children during aarti and queueing; strollers may need to wait outside the main hall during peak crowds.

For detailed accessibility notes, see Visit Us.

What to bring & what to avoid

Bring: modest attire, footwear you can remove easily, a small offering if you wish (flowers, fruits - confirm with office), and patience during busy hours.

Avoid: loud phone use in the sanctum, strong fragrances that may disturb others, large bags during crowded aartis, and photography without office permission.
