Community seva

CONCEPTUAL

Volunteer at the temple

Seva is offered through your time, care, and presence - not through this website. All volunteering is coordinated directly with the temple office.

CONCEPTUAL Contact the office first

There is no online volunteer signup or payment on bellandursaibabatemple.com.

Introduce yourself to the managing committee by email or in person before serving. They will explain current needs, dress and etiquette, and any temple-specific guidelines for Annadanam, festivals, and daily support.

How you can help

Ways to volunteer

Families, students, and neighbours from Bellandur and beyond contribute in many forms - always under the committee’s direction.

Annadanam

Help with meal preparation, serving prasadam, and clearing after Thursday and festival-day Annadanam. Many volunteers serve together as families.

Annadanam details

Festivals & Thursdays

Assist with crowd guidance, seating for elders, flower arrangements, and setup on busy observances when the committee requests extra hands.

Festival calendar

Temple upkeep

Quiet support for cleanliness, decoration, and day-to-day tasks - coordinated so worship in the sanctum remains peaceful and unhurried.

About the temple

Skills & professions

Occasionally the committee welcomes specific skills (for example electrical, plumbing, or printing) for maintenance projects - always discussed in advance with the office.

Annadanam

Food as sacred offering

Annadanam volunteers wash vegetables, arrange plates, serve meals after darshan, and help clear respectfully afterward. Children may assist with adult supervision when the committee approves.

Sponsoring a day’s meal is arranged with the office in person - not through online payment on this site.

Annadanam page Contact the office

Transparency

What this page does not provide

  • Online payment, donation links, or UPI details of any kind.
  • Guaranteed volunteer slots on specific dates without office confirmation.
  • Background checks or formal HR processes - the committee knows its regular volunteers by fellowship.
  • Tax receipts or legal documentation - obtain only what the committee confirms when you enquire directly.

Ready to offer your time?

Email the temple office or visit during open hours to introduce yourself. We are grateful for every act of seva offered with shraddha and saburi.
