Getting here

Transport & traffic

Bellandur is well connected to the Outer Ring Road (ORR) and Sarjapur Road. Routes and congestion change frequently - check your maps app for live traffic before you leave.

Bellandur area: The temple sits in Green Glen Layout, near the Bellandur lake and ORR corridor. By car or cab, search for “Margosa Avenue, Green Glen Layout” or the temple name. By bus or metro, use the stop nearest your starting point and plan the last leg with a maps app - there is no dedicated temple shuttle.

  • Allow extra time on Thursday evenings and festival days when roads and temple surroundings are busier.
  • Street parking is available in the layout; please park considerately and follow any guidance from volunteers on busy days.
  • Ride-hailing drop-off at the layout entrance is usually easiest; confirm the pin on the map above.
  • We do not publish fixed public-transit routes here because services change - your maps app will have current options.

Before you arrive

Visit checklist

A short guide for first-time visitors and families - especially helpful when visiting with elders.

Plan your timing

  • Check daily aarti timings - Kakada (6:30 AM), Madhyana (12 PM), Dhoopaarathi (6:30 PM), Shejaarathi (9 PM).
  • Temple generally opens around 6:00 AM; confirm on festival days with the office.
  • Thursday evenings draw larger crowds - arrive early for Dhoopaarathi if you prefer a quieter darshan.

Etiquette & comfort

  • Dress modestly; remove footwear before entering the shrine area.
  • Maintain silence in the darshan hall; phones on silent or vibrate.
  • Volunteers can help elders find seating - please ask at the entrance.

Offerings & sevas

  • Flowers and prasadam offerings are welcome as per temple guidance.
  • Book sevas in advance at the office or by email.
  • Indicative seva rates are on the website; final amounts are confirmed in person.

Community seva

  • Interested in Annadanam or volunteering? Contact the office before your visit.
  • This website does not accept online payments - arrangements are made directly with the temple.
  • See ways to volunteer for general guidance (conceptual overview).
