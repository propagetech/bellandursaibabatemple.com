Our temple

Faith, fellowship, and service in Bellandur

Shri Sai Baba Temple welcomes families, elders, and first-time visitors from across Bengaluru’s IT corridor for peaceful darshan and community worship.

From Bhoomi Puja to daily aarti, our volunteers sustain a warm gathering place rooted in Sai Baba’s teachings of shraddha (faith) and saburi (patience). Temple history and milestone dates are maintained by the managing committee and updated here as verified.

Community seva

Annadanam - food as sacred offering

Annadanam at Shri Sai Baba Temple nourishes devotees after darshan, especially on Thursdays and festival days. Families and volunteers may contribute ingredients, service time, or sponsor a full day’s meal with the temple office.

Sponsorship amounts and scheduling are arranged in person or by email - we do not accept online payments on this website.
