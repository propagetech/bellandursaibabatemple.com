How to book

Booking via email or temple office

By email

Write to saibaba.bellandur@gmail.com with your name, mobile number, preferred seva, and tentative date. The office will reply when volunteers are available - allow a few business days.

Visit the temple office

During open hours (generally 6:00 AM–9:30 PM), speak with the office at 109 Margosa Ave, Green Glen Layout, Bellandur. Bring ID for special poojas; pay as directed by the committee (cash or UPI at the office).

Directions & visiting tips

What to include in your enquiry

  • Seva name (for example, Abhishekam or Pallakki Seva)
  • Preferred date and whether it is a Thursday or festival day
  • Names for archana, if applicable
  • Any dietary or prasadam distribution needs

Guidance

Etiquette & disclaimers

  • Dress modestly; remove footwear before entering the sanctum area.
  • Arrive at least fifteen minutes before your scheduled seva unless the office advises otherwise.
  • Photography and mobile use may be restricted during pooja - follow volunteer instructions.
  • Seva slots may be rescheduled on festival days or during temple maintenance.
  • Donations are voluntary offerings; receipts are issued as per temple policy.
  • This page is for information only - not a contract of service or guaranteed availability.
